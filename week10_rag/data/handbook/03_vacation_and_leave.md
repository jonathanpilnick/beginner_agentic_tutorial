# Vacation and Sick Leave

> FICTIONAL COMPANY. Classroom exercise only.

## Vacation days

- In your first year you receive **18 vacation days**.
- You receive one extra day for every additional year at Nimbus, up to a maximum of **24 days** per year.
- You may carry over up to **5 unused days** into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by **March 31**, or they expire.

Request vacation in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

## Sick leave

If you are sick, notify your manager before 10:00 on the same day. A doctor's note is required after **3 consecutive sick days**.

## Public holidays

The office is closed on Israeli public holidays. The full calendar is published in the HR portal every December.
