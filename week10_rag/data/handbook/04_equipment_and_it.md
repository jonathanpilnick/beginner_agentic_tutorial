# Laptops, Equipment and IT

> FICTIONAL COMPANY. Classroom exercise only.

## Your laptop

New employees choose between a 14-inch MacBook Pro and a Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are replaced every **36 months**. If your laptop breaks earlier, the IT help desk will repair or replace it.

## Lost or stolen equipment

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT help desk **within 24 hours**. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. You will not be charged for a first loss.

## Accounts and passwords

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for every company account. Store passwords in the company password manager, KeyNest. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff: IT will never ask for it.

## VPN

Use the company VPN whenever you access internal systems from outside the office.
