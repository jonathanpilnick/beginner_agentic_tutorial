# Welcome to Nimbus Robotics

> FICTIONAL COMPANY. This handbook was invented for a classroom exercise. None of these policies are real.

Nimbus Robotics builds small warehouse robots that move boxes between shelves. The company was founded in 2019 and has about 140 employees.

## Who to contact

- People team (HR questions): people@nimbus-robotics.example
- IT help desk: it-help@nimbus-robotics.example, or extension 4357 ("HELP" on a phone keypad)
- Security team: security@nimbus-robotics.example

## Your first week

On your first day you will receive your laptop, your office badge and a buddy from your team. Your buddy is your go-to person for informal questions during your first 90 days.

The onboarding checklist must be completed by the end of your second week.
