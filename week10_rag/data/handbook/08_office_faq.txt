NIMBUS ROBOTICS - OFFICE FAQ
(FICTIONAL COMPANY. Classroom exercise only.)

Q: Is there parking at the office?
A: Yes. There are 40 parking spaces in the underground garage. They are first come, first served. Bicycle racks are next to the main entrance.

Q: Are dogs allowed in the office?
A: Dogs are welcome on Fridays only, and they must stay on a leash in shared areas.

Q: Is there a gym subsidy?
A: Yes. Nimbus reimburses up to 150 NIS per month for a gym or sports club membership.

Q: What time does the office open?
A: The building is open from 07:00 to 21:00 on Sunday to Thursday. On Friday it closes at 14:00.

Q: Who refills the kitchen?
A: The office manager orders snacks and fruit every Sunday and Wednesday.
