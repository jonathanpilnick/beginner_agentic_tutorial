# Expenses and Learning Budget

> FICTIONAL COMPANY. Classroom exercise only.

## Submitting expenses

Submit every expense in the SpendLog app with a photo of the receipt **within 30 days** of the purchase. Expenses above **1,000 NIS** need your manager's approval before you buy.

## Business travel

During business travel abroad, meals are covered up to **75 USD per day**. Book flights through the travel desk; economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of **3,000 NIS per calendar year** for books, online courses and conference tickets. Unused learning budget does not carry over to the next year.
