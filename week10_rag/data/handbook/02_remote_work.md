# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

> FICTIONAL COMPANY. Classroom exercise only.

Last updated: January 2026. This policy replaces the 2022 remote work policy.

## How many days can I work from home?

Employees may work remotely up to **2 days per week**. Tuesday and Wednesday are "anchor days": everyone is expected in the office on those days so teams can meet in person.

## Core hours

When working remotely you must be reachable on chat and video between **10:00 and 15:00** (Israel time). Outside core hours you can organise your day freely.

## Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to **20 working days per calendar year**. This requires written approval from your manager at least two weeks in advance.

## Home office equipment

Nimbus provides a one-time home office allowance of 1,200 NIS for a chair, monitor or desk. Submit the receipt through the expense process.
