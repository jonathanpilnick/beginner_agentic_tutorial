# Information Security and AI Tools

> FICTIONAL COMPANY. Classroom exercise only.

## Data classification

Nimbus data has three levels: Public, Internal and Confidential. Customer data, warehouse maps and source code are **Confidential**.

## Using AI assistants

- You may use the company's approved AI workspace for Internal data.
- **Never paste Confidential data, including customer data, into personal or unapproved AI tools.**
- When in doubt, ask the security team before sharing data with any external service.

## Phishing

Forward suspicious emails to security@nimbus-robotics.example and then delete them. Do not click links or open attachments in a suspicious email.
