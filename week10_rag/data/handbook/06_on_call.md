# On-Call Policy for Engineers

> FICTIONAL COMPANY. Classroom exercise only.

## Rotation

Engineers in the Platform and Robotics teams join a weekly on-call rotation. A shift runs from Monday 10:00 to the following Monday 10:00.

## Response times

| Severity | Example | Acknowledge within |
|---|---|---|
| SEV1 | Robots stopped in a customer warehouse | 15 minutes |
| SEV2 | One robot keeps failing | 1 hour |
| SEV3 | Dashboard bug | Next business day |

## Compensation

Each full on-call week is paid **900 NIS**. If you were paged between 00:00 and 06:00, you receive a **day off** within the following two weeks.
